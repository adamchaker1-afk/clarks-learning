Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 12, 2026

The Clarks Learning Uploader only stores authorization tokens for the owner's own accounts. It holds no data about other people. To remove access and stored data:

Remove access on each platform

  • Google / YouTube: open your Google Account security settings, go to third-party access, select the app, and choose to remove access.
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app, open Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps and services, and remove the app.
  • Instagram / Meta: in Instagram settings, open Apps and websites, and remove the app.

Request deletion

To ask that any stored tokens or related records be deleted, email adamchaker1@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". Requests are handled within 30 days.
