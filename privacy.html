Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy explains how the Clarks Learning Uploader ("the Tool"), operated by Adam Chaker ("I", "me"), handles information. The Tool is a private application used only by its owner to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own social media accounts.

Who uses the Tool

The Tool is not available to the public. The only person who signs in to it is the owner. It does not collect information about visitors, subscribers, viewers, or any other users of YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram.

Information the Tool accesses

When the owner connects an account, the Tool receives:

  • An authorization token from the platform (Google, TikTok, or Meta), which lets the Tool upload videos to the owner's account.
  • Basic profile information about the owner's own account, such as its display name, as needed to confirm which account is connected.
  • The titles, descriptions, and tags of videos the Tool publishes, which the owner wrote or approved.

How the information is used

This information is used only to upload and schedule the owner's own videos. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

Authorization tokens are stored on the owner's own computer or server and are not shared with anyone. I do not sell, rent, or trade information, and I do not share it with third parties, except that the Tool sends videos and their titles and descriptions to the platform the owner chose to post to.

Google and YouTube

The Tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and use of Google data is subject to the Google Privacy Policy. The owner can revoke the Tool's access at any time in the Google security settings.

TikTok

The Tool uses TikTok's developer services only to post the owner's own videos to the owner's own account. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok's app settings under security and permissions for connected apps.

Instagram and Meta

The Tool uses Meta's APIs only to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own professional Instagram account. Access can be removed at any time in Instagram's settings under apps and websites.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the owner keeps the account connected. Revoking access on the platform, or deleting the Tool's stored credentials, ends access and removes the stored tokens. See the data deletion instructions.

Children

The Tool is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information about them.

Changes

I may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact
