Clarks Learning

Short-form educational videos

Clarks Learning is a personal educational video project run by Adam Chaker. It publishes short tutorials and explainers cut from the owner's own long-form recordings.

About the Clarks Learning Uploader

The Clarks Learning Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner. It prepares the owner's own videos and posts them to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no one other than the owner signs in to it.
